# Getting help with Logistico

- **Questions, ideas, "how do I…?"**: ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/Haix02/logistico-software/discussions).
  Arabic or English are both fine.
- **Something is broken**: open a [bug report](https://github.com/Haix02/logistico-software/issues/new/choose) with
  what you did, what you expected and what happened (screenshots help).
- **A feature your company needs**: open a [feature request](https://github.com/Haix02/logistico-software/issues/new/choose)
  and describe the work it would save.
- **A security problem**: never in public; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before asking, the [documentation](https://haix02.github.io/logistico-software/) may already answer it: the
[overview](docs/00-overview.md), [first run and getting started](docs/19-first-run-and-getting-started.md) and
[production readiness](docs/15-production-readiness.md) cover most installation questions.
